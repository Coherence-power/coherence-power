For owners and managers of energy-intensive plants

Lower your plant’s power bill or add capacity. See the return before you spend.

We model your plant from its drawings, two months of bills and one walk through the floor. No meters to install. Then we try the investment in the model first, so you see what it should return before you spend anything.

  • Cutting energy costs?

    You get the yearly running cost of every major pump, blower and compressor, plus a list of fixes sorted by payback. The first few are often schedule or setpoint changes that cost nothing.

  • Planning an expansion?

    We add the new line, a battery or on-site generation to the model and run it against your tariff and your current grid connection. You get an estimate of how much more the plant can run before you ask the utility for an upgrade.

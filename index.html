The utility bill gives you one number for the whole plant. The decisions you have to make are about individual machines.

How we get from your drawings to a cost for every machine

  1. Scan the process

    You send P&IDs, an equipment list and two months of bills and interval data. Then we walk the floor with your team, read nameplates, and ask operators how each unit is really run.

  2. Build the plant in software

    We model the process from the physics up: pump curves, motors, pressure drops, tank volumes. We then tune it until its total tracks your utility meter, so the breakdown inside it can be trusted.

  3. Find where the money goes

    The model shows what each machine costs to run and what it would cost if it ran well. The gap between the two is your list: pumps off their curve, equipment left idling, loads running in the most expensive hours.

  4. Fix what's worth fixing

    We rank every fix by savings and payback and help you carry it out. Some are a setpoint or schedule change your operators can make this week. Others are capital projects, and the model gives you the numbers to take to finance.

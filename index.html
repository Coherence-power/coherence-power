01 / The problem

The savings are already in your plant. They're just hard to reach.

Your pumps, compressors, tanks and batch steps have room to move. Finding out how much, without risking production, has been the hard part.

A

Rates got complicated

Time-of-use pricing, demand charges and grid programs mean every hour of operation now costs something different, and the rules keep changing.

B

Studies are slow and expensive

A rigorous energy study can take many months and cost hundreds of thousands of dollars, and it's out of date the next time equipment or rates change.

C

Generic programs don't know your process

Enrolling in a demand-response program doesn't guarantee savings. Blunt curtailment that ignores process constraints puts quality and throughput at risk.

02 / How it works

A digital twin of your plant, so you focus on production, not worrying about energy costs.

We model how your facility actually runs, from equipment and process limits to your tariff, then search for the cheapest way to make the same product.

  1. Step 01

    Connect

    Share your utility bills, interval meter data and process data from SCADA or your historian. No new hardware to start.

  2. Step 02

    Model

    We build an energy digital twin: your equipment, process constraints, production targets and rate structure in one model.

  3. Step 03

    Optimize

    We test thousands of operating configurations, from schedules and setpoints to storage sizing, and rank them by savings and payback.

  4. Step 04

    Operate

    A co-pilot recommends day-ahead schedules. The model re-optimizes automatically when equipment, production or rates change.

The rigor of an engineering study. The speed and cost of software.

  • 10×

    Faster than a traditional study. We automate the analyst work, so you see answers in weeks, not quarters.

  • Millions

    Of scenarios scanned. Risk-aware optimization finds plans that hold up when prices spike or markets shift, not just on the expected day.

  • Always

    Up to date. Models re-optimize when you add equipment, change production or your utility changes rates.

03 / Your process comes first

Savings that never cost you a spec.

Every recommendation is built around the limits your plant already runs on. Product delivery and quality are fixed inputs, never trade-offs.

  • Product quality

    Spec limits and quality targets are hard constraints in the model.

  • Production targets

    Daily volumes and delivery commitments stay fixed. We only move when you make it.

  • Equipment health

    Start/stop limits, ramp rates, pressures and tank levels are modeled explicitly.

  • Your operators decide

    Clear recommendations your team can review, not a black box that takes over the controls.
